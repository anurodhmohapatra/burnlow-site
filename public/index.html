THE PROBLEM

Why Databricks bills creep

Most waste isn't exotic. It's sensible defaults applied to the wrong workloads, and nobody owning the bill.

Photon where it doesn't pay

On light or I/O-bound jobs, you may pay a higher DBU rate without getting faster results.

Production jobs on interactive compute

All-purpose clusters can cost more than jobs compute. Pipelines sometimes land on them by accident.

Oversized and idle clusters

Clusters sized for last year's peak, missing auto-termination, or running on-demand unnecessarily.

Serverless without guardrails

Without usage policies and budgets, convenient serverless compute can hide runaway spend.

Spend nobody owns

Without tags and attribution, teams cannot identify which workloads drive the bill.

What we won't charge you for

Native table maintenance, budgets and cost dashboards. We focus on decisions tools cannot make for you.
