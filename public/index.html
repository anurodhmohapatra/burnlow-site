The problem

Why Databricks bills creep

Most of the waste isn't exotic. It's sensible defaults applied to the wrong workloads, and nobody owning the bill.

Photon configuration

Photon can improve performance, but the cost/performance trade-off varies by workload. We benchmark eligible workloads rather than recommending a blanket on/off setting.

Job and interactive compute

We check whether scheduled workloads use an appropriate compute type and configuration for their runtime, concurrency and operational requirements.

Cluster sizing and idle time

We look for over-provisioning, low utilisation, long idle periods and auto-termination settings that may be increasing spend.

Serverless usage controls

We review workload policies, budgets and usage patterns to identify where guardrails or configuration changes may be warranted.

Cost attribution

We check whether tags and available system-table metadata make spend attributable to teams, jobs and environments.

What we won't charge you for

We won't repackage built-in platform dashboards as consulting. The sprint focuses on interpreting your workload and cost data, prioritising changes, and helping your team validate the result.

What you get

Fixes, not a findings deck

A report alone doesn't lower a bill. Every sprint ends with changes your engineers can review and ship.

  1. Cost map. Spend by workspace, team, job and compute type, to the extent supported by available billing and system-table metadata.
  2. Ranked opportunities. Findings ranked by estimated impact, implementation effort, operational risk and confidence.
  3. Engineering changes. Proposed configuration changes, code/config examples and implementation notes for the agreed priority workloads.
  4. Change plan. A sequenced plan for your team to review and execute under its own change controls.
  5. 30-day validation. A before-and-after comparison using an agreed measurement method and comparable workloads.
  6. Executive summary. A concise summary of findings, assumptions, actions and measured impact.

FAQ

Questions teams ask first

Do you need admin or write access?

No. Read-only access to billing, compute and job metadata is enough. We never change anything in your workspace; your team ships every change.

How much can we expect to save?

We won't quote a percentage before reviewing your workload and billing data. The calculator is an illustrative scenario, not a forecast. After the fit call, we'll tell you whether a deeper assessment is likely to be worthwhile.

How are savings measured?

Before work begins, we agree on a baseline, eligible workloads and a measurement method. After your team implements the agreed changes, we compare a representative 30-day period and document material differences or limitations. Any annualised figure is a run-rate estimate, not a promise of 12 months of identical savings.

Does the sprint guarantee savings?

The engagement includes a 50% fee-refund commitment if verified annualised savings run-rate is below three times the sprint fee, subject to the baseline, implementation and measurement conditions agreed in writing before the sprint.

How long does it take?

The sprint itself takes two to three weeks. We re-measure 30 days after your team ships the changes.

Who implements the changes?

Your engineers approve and implement changes under your own controls. The sprint provides prioritised recommendations, implementation notes and review support; production changes are not made by Burnlow.

Are you a Databricks partner?

No. Burnlow is independent and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Databricks. We don't sell licences, migrations or credits.

Do you work with Snowflake, BigQuery or EMR?

Databricks is our focus. Spark on AWS EMR or Glue is possible on request.

Do you offer on-call or production support?

No. We're an analysis and implementation-support practice, not a managed-service provider.

How do payments work?

50% at kickoff and 50% on delivery, by bank transfer, UPI or card. International teams are billed in USD.
