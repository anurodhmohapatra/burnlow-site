The problem

Why Databricks bills creep

Most of the waste isn't exotic. It's sensible defaults applied to the wrong workloads, and nobody owning the bill.

Photon where it doesn't pay

Photon is on by default and bills at a higher DBU rate. On light or I/O-bound jobs, you pay more without going faster.

Production jobs on interactive compute

All-purpose clusters cost more per DBU than jobs compute, and it's easy to schedule pipelines on them by accident.

Oversized and idle clusters

Sized for last year's peak, missing auto-termination, or running on-demand where spot workers would do.

Serverless without guardrails

Serverless is convenient. Without usage policies and budgets, it's also where runaway spend hides.

Spend nobody owns

Without tags and attribution, no one can say which team or pipeline is driving the bill, so nothing changes.

What we won't charge you for

Databricks now automates table maintenance and ships budgets and cost dashboards for free. We focus on the decisions a tool can't make for you.
