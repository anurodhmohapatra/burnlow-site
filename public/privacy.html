Privacy

Last updated 18 November 2026

This website

This website does not use advertising pixels or third-party marketing trackers, and No Cloudflare Web Analytics script is installed on this site, and Cloudflare Real User Monitoring is disabled for this zone. The site is delivered through Cloudflare, which may process technical request data to provide caching, security and delivery. The contact form creates a pre-filled email in your email application; the website itself does not receive or store the form fields. If you send that email, we receive and retain the message in our email system to respond to and manage the enquiry.

When you email us

We use your name, email address and anything you tell us only to reply and arrange a call. We don't sell or share it, and we don't add you to a mailing list.

Client engagements

Engagements run under a signed NDA. We work with read-only access to billing, compute and job metadata, and don't need customer or business data. Extracts are deleted at the end of the engagement unless the client asks us in writing to keep them.

Your rights

We handle personal data in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, email hello@burnlow.in.
